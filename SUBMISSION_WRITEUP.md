# Dubai Mall Interactive Sales Deck - Brief Write-Up

## Design Rationale

The assignment asked for more than a static mall website, so I treated the project as an interactive commercial sales deck. The goal was to help a leasing, sponsorship, or events team create immediate emotional buy-in and then guide a prospect toward a business conversation.

I selected Dubai Mall because it naturally supports the strongest themes in the brief: global scale, luxury retail, tourism, entertainment, events, sponsorship, and cinematic destination storytelling. A conventional mall presentation could easily become informational. Dubai Mall allowed the experience to feel aspirational, premium, and commercially urgent.

The first screen is intentionally video-led. The opening message frames the property as a destination platform rather than a shopping center:

> Not a mall. The stage where global brands become moments.

That framing drives the rest of the experience. Each section is designed to answer a commercial question:

- Why is this property valuable?
- Which prospect is in the room: tenant, sponsor, or event producer?
- What kind of audience and attention does the property offer?
- How can a brand activate inside the destination?
- What is the next business action?

The visual direction uses a dark luxury palette, large typography, cinematic spacing, soft gold accents, and restrained motion. I avoided making it feel like a dashboard or template website because the brief evaluates storytelling, polish, and sales impact.

## Experience Strategy

The deck is structured like a live pitch:

1. Open with cinematic emotion and scale.
2. Prove the destination's business gravity with metrics.
3. Let the salesperson choose the buyer path.
4. Show luxury, entertainment, sponsorship, and event opportunities.
5. Include one memorable interactive feature that makes the pitch feel custom.
6. End with a clear commercial call to action.

The standout feature is the Brand Lab and sponsor activation showcase. These modules help a prospect imagine their own brand inside the property instead of only reading about sponsorship inventory. This makes the project feel more like a sales tool than a brochure.

## How I Used AI

I used AI as a creative and strategic accelerator across research, positioning, and concept development:

- Interpreted the assignment brief and evaluation priorities.
- Compared possible mall subjects and selected Dubai Mall for strongest fit.
- Developed the sales narrative and section flow.
- Explored premium visual directions, motion references, and presentation pacing.
- Created a 45-second cinematic mega-mall reel concept for the hero and reel sections.
- Created concept visual assets for venue, sponsorship, and leasing modules.
- Drafted and refined the recruiter-facing README and this write-up.

AI was used to improve the product thinking: positioning, pacing, visual storytelling, commercial messaging, and handoff documentation.

All generated visuals are treated as concept assets. In a real client production environment, I would replace or supplement them with licensed Dubai Mall media, approved brand assets, official venue data, and production-ready video exports.

## Technical Approach

The project is built as a static site using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I chose this approach because it keeps the project fast, portable, and easy for reviewers to run without setup.

Key technical choices:

- Local MP4 video for reliable playback.
- CSS-driven luxury visual system.
- Dependency-free smooth scrolling.
- Scroll-triggered reveal animations.
- Animated number counters.
- Hero video parallax.
- Interactive tabs for audience, platform, modules, and itineraries.
- Responsive desktop and tablet layout.
- Static deployment support for Netlify, Vercel, and GitHub Pages.

The code is organized to support future modular expansion through `data/deck-data.js`, reusable section patterns, and clear asset folders.

## What I Would Improve With More Time

With additional time, I would take the project closer to production quality by adding:

- Compressed WebM and mobile-specific video variants.
- Lighthouse testing after deployment with verified 90+ performance score.
- Official Dubai Mall photography, video, and floor-plan data.
- Real venue capacity and booking information.
- Sponsor package tiers with pricing logic and exportable summaries.
- Analytics to track which sections prospects view most.
- A downloadable leave-behind PDF generated from selected modules.
- Deeper event-booking and leasing workflows.

## Closing Thought

My intention was to show that I can think beyond implementation and build a premium commercial experience. The final deck combines frontend craft, visual taste, AI-assisted production, storytelling, and business strategy into one focused submission.
